Ciao! My name is Andrea.
‍
I help businesses and institutions transform the way they work, build amazing customer solutions, keep their audience engaged, and find value—all this by developing and executing strategies for digital products and experiences through innovative and conscious use of data.

I am Design Director at Accurat: from our offices in Milan & New York we do all of the above, for clients across the world, often in collaboration with global design consultancy leader Pentagram.

->->->

Q: Ok, but what exactly do you do?
‍
A: In my work, I combine strategic and managerial skills with a background as data-driven solutions specialist to drive digital innovation at international corporations, startups, cultural institutions and NGOs—helping them leverage data to solve complex problems.

First as Designer and currently as Manager, I led the development, launch and growth of dozens of custom solutions for corporations, such as web and mobile applications, business intelligence dashboards, websites, financial and sustainability reports, as well as data-driven media campaigns and installations.
